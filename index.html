WE ARE THE PACK

The Howlers are an FTC robotics team built on a simple belief: when a pack hunts together, nothing is out of reach. We design, build, and program robots to compete at the highest levels of FIRST Tech Challenge.

From the first sketch to the last match, every member of our team brings their unique strengths. Engineers, programmers, strategists, and builders — all running as one.

We don't just build robots. We build problem-solvers, innovators, and leaders who will carry that pack mentality into every challenge they face.

// 02 — Our Pack

PACK VALUES

01
Loyalty
We show up for each other. Every member of the pack matters, and no one is left behind. Our strength comes from unity.
02
Ingenuity
Every problem is a puzzle waiting to be solved. We approach challenges with creativity, iteration, and an unwillingness to settle.
03
Tenacity
Robots break. Code fails. We keep going. The Howlers push through setbacks because that's how champions are forged.
04
Gracious Professionalism
We compete hard and we help others. FIRST's core values guide everything we do, on and off the field.
05
Impact
We build robots, but our real goal is to inspire. Through outreach and mentorship, we grow the next generation of engineers.
06
Excellence
Good enough isn't in our vocabulary. Every subsystem, every line of code, every match — we give it everything.
